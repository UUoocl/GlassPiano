# Specification: Enhance Hand Tracking and Synchronization

## Objective
Improve the core AR piano experience by enhancing the precision of MediaPipe hand tracking and ensuring seamless synchronization with OpenSheetMusicDisplay rendering.

## Requirements
- Optimize MediaPipe configuration for lower latency and better finger recognition.
- Improve synchronization between detected keystrokes and sheet music progress.
- Maintain unobtrusive, minimalist UI overlays per Product Guidelines.
- Ensure all logic adheres to Atomic Design principles and keyboard accessibility.
